## 2. Security & Integrity Risks Identified

1. **Non-Compliant Communication Channels & Webhooks:** `whatsappRoutes.js` exposes `/api/whatsapp/webhook`, `/api/whatsapp/*`, and `authRoutes.js` exposes SMS/WhatsApp OTP endpoints. Removing these shrinks the external attack surface and removes unused credential surfaces (`WHATSAPP_APP_SECRET`, `TWILIO_AUTH_TOKEN`, `AT_API_KEY`).
2. **Unenforced Business Admin Onboarding:** Newly created businesses assigned to Business Admins are not guided through required setup or gated from operational routes prior to setup completion.
3. **Unverified External WhatsApp Numbers:** Businesses and users can currently specify arbitrary `whatsapp_number` fields decoupled from verified/registered phone numbers (`SettingsPage.jsx`, `businessRoutes.js`, `PublicPages.jsx`).
4. **Inconsistent Email-Change OTP TTL:** `/api/auth/request-email-change` uses a 20-minute TTL (`authRoutes.js:1524`) instead of the mandated 5-minute TTL.
5. **Missing Operational Telemetry & DB Backup:** Failed auth lockouts, permission denials, and unhandled 500s are not aggregated into a queryable `monitoring_events` feed with Super Admin alerting, and Super Admins lack a one-click DB backup export.
